- (constraint) One persistent roster spans WoW tiers; only one raid tier is active at a time.
- (constraint) Owner alone edits Sheets and uses the enhanced Gargul interface; raiders have read-only Sheets access and need no addon installation.
- (constraint) Gargul enhancement displays roll value, BiS Ratio, and Impact Score but has no item-type knowledge and does not decide which signal applies or award loot; owner consults Sheets and decides.
- (constraint) BiS Ratio uses attendance since its three-week reset; Impact Score uses attendance across the whole active tier. Presence Max starts at 1 when its window has no possible raids.
- (constraint) Owner imports each raid export once and manually corrects JSON when needed; the system rejects malformed JSON without mutating existing data but does not deduplicate uploads.
- (non-goal) Managing multiple guilds or rosters, automatically assigning loot, addon item classification, automated BiS/parse ingestion, and Tank Impact Scores are outside MVP.

- (decision) Allocation companion: item classes are BiS, Impact, Tier Set, Class-Bound, and normal; Tier Set/Class-Bound roll among eligible classes after MT1 then MT2 tank priority. BiS-eligible rollers outrank non-BiS regardless of ratio; BiS candidates rank by (BiS Presence/BiS Presence Max)/(BiS loots since reset+1), exact ties use Gargul roll, no-BiS candidates roll without ratio impact, and BiS cannot be passed. Marking a BiS award Impact leaves BiS Ratio unchanged and updates Impact state. Impact Score=(Tier Presence/Tier Presence Max)*DPS Warcraft Logs average parse*simmed percent DPS/HPS gain/(Impact Item Count+1); missing parse=1; tanks have no Impact metric; identical scores tie by roll. BiS attendance and loot count reset three weeks into a tier; Impact Count carryover is owner-configurable. Archives are read-only/non-restorable; rare class exceptions remain manual.
- (decision) Raid/data companion: owner manually maintains roster, Impact Items, parses, and per-phase BiS in Sheets; attendance comes from Warcraft Logs raid links, bench is manual, unmatched players are ignored/corrected by owner. Raiders view three read-only tabs: roster/Presence/BiS Ratio; Impact priority with score breakdown; per-spec item slots/status/gain. Owner-only Gargul enhancement imports Sheet JSON before raid, shows Gargul roll type/value plus ratios/scores without item classification or award automation, updates values live for subsequent rolls, and exports all attributions for one manual post-raid import. Impact checkbox on a BiS award updates Impact state only. Malformed JSON is rejected without state changes; no duplicate-upload check, owner imports once and corrects JSON manually. V1 is Sheets plus owner Lua/Gargul; desktop/local-JSON GUI is future preference.
